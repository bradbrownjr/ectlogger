# Net control

This path is for the people running the net while it is happening. In ECTLogger that is three per-net roles, held for one net at a time and given up when it closes:

- **NCS**, Net Control Station: runs the net. Starts it, works the list, decides what happens, closes it.
- **Logger**: types. Everything Net Control can do to a check-in, the Logger can do too. On a busy net this is the difference between running the net and transcribing it.
- **Relay**: checks in stations on behalf of others, for the stations Net Control cannot hear directly.

These are not the same as your account's global role. A per-net role applies to one net and nothing else, which is why a brand new account can be handed Net Control on somebody else's net without being given the run of the site. The full breakdown is in [roles and permissions](/docs/reference/roles-and-permissions/).

## What you will be able to do

Take a net from lobby to closed without touching anything else: log stations as fast as they call, correct a row without losing your place, work several frequencies at once alongside other net controls, keep the chat civil, confirm a station is who it says it is, handle formal traffic, spread the desk across as many screens as you have, and close out with the log already written.

## Before your first net

Two things are worth doing once, in advance, rather than discovering mid-net:

1. **Find out whether you can actually take control.** If you are on the net staff of the schedule the net comes from, you can take Net Control yourself by checking yourself in as NCS, even on a week you are not scheduled. If you aren't, someone already running the net has to give you the role. [Roles and stepping away](/docs/net-control/roles-and-stepping-away/) covers which case you are in.
2. **Look at the desk once when nothing is happening.** The net view has a lot on it, and the first time you see it should not be with eleven stations calling. [The net control desk](/docs/net-control/the-net-control-desk/) is a tour with the controls labeled.
