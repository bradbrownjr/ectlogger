# The ICS-214 activity log

The ICS-214 Activity Log is the Incident Command System (ICS) form that records what a unit did: who was assigned, and the notable things that happened, with times. A served agency files it, and it's the reference for the after-action report. ECTLogger fills it in from the net's log, so you start from a finished draft instead of a blank form.

It's for whoever can close the net: the NCS, the Logger, the net's manager, and the manager of the schedule it came from. It opens once the net has closed, including while you're [finishing the log later](/docs/net-control/closing-the-net/#finishing-the-log-later).

## Fill it in and download it

1. On the closed net, click **Reports** on the toolbar and choose **ICS-214**.
2. Check the boxes at the top. ECTLogger fills in **1. Incident name** (the drill plan's exercise name, or the net's name), **2. From** and **To** (when the net ran), **3. Name** (the net's schedule, or its own name when it has none), **4. ICS position** (whoever was credited as NCS), and **8. Prepared by** (you). Type your team into **5. Home agency (and unit)**, such as Example County ARES.
3. Under **6. Resources assigned**, check the list of everyone who took part. Fill in an ICS position or home agency where you know it, and untick **On form** for anyone who doesn't belong on it.
4. Under **7. Activity log**, read the lines. Untick **On form** to leave one off, or click the pencil to reword it.
5. Under **Add a line**, enter the date and time and what happened, then click **Add**, for anything that happened off the air: a briefing, a site being set up, a supply run.
6. Click **Download PDF**. Your changes are saved first.

**Save** keeps your changes without downloading. A line you added is saved as soon as you click **Add**, and so is a change to it; everything else waits for **Save** or **Download PDF**, and your browser asks before you close the tab with changes unsaved.

## What goes on the form

Every line comes from something that was logged on the net, never from the schedule's roster or the rotation:

- The net opening, on its frequencies, and closing.
- A station on the air from a place for the first time, such as "on the air from Naples Shelter on 146.520 FM", and each time it moves somewhere new, with where it moved from. A station checked in In person reads "checked in in person".
- A station adding a channel at a place. A recheck on a channel it already had is left out, since it isn't news.
- A channel [marked offline](/docs/net-control/logging-check-ins/#a-stations-channels), and back online.
- A station checking out on its own. A station that moved, or that was still on when the net closed, isn't listed as checking out.
- Someone taking net control, logging, or relay, if they took part in the net.
- Each step of a formal message's handling, as on the ICS-309: what it was and who handled it, never the message text.
- The lines you added.

Times are on the 24-hour clock, in your own time zone, or UTC if your profile prefers it. The date is added when the net ran over more than one day, or when a line falls on another day.

A station added from a paper log with no time shows **Not recorded** here and prints with a blank time.

## The parts that surprise people

**"On the air from", not "arrived".** A check-in shows the station was on the air from that place at that time. It doesn't show when the team got there, so the form doesn't claim it. Reword a line if you know more.

**Your changes stay put; the rest follows the log.** A box you didn't change keeps following the net, so fixing the net's start time or the drill plan's exercise name updates the form too. A line you reworded keeps your wording.

**A drill is marked on every page.** On a net with a [drill plan](/docs/net-managers/drill-plans/), each page of the PDF says THIS IS AN EXERCISE at its top and bottom, so a printed copy found later can't be taken for a real incident.

**ECTLogger doesn't keep the copy you handed over.** Downloading again makes a new PDF from the form as it is now. Keep the file you gave the agency.
