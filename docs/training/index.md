# Training

The rest of this site is written to be read on your own, one task at a time. Training is the other way in: somebody showing you, start to finish, while you watch or follow along. This page collects what exists for that and what is on the way.

**Most of it is still being made.** The video series is planned and its graphics are built, but the recordings are not posted yet, and there are no slide decks here yet. What is listed below as planned is planned, not available. Check back, or subscribe to the channel.

## Upcoming sessions

Live sessions, in person or online, are posted in the app rather than here, because the app can show them in your own time zone and take them down once they are over. Open **Help** in the top bar, then **Training**. If a session has an online meeting link, sign in to see it.

You don't have to go looking, either. From the start of the day a week before a session, a purple banner across the top of the main pages announces it, says whether you can attend in person, online, or both, and opens the details with **More info**. Close it with the **✕** once you've seen it; when you're signed in, it stays closed on your other devices too.

Running a session for your own club? An administrator can post it in the same place: see [Training sessions](/docs/admins/training-sessions/).

## Videos

The [ECTLogger channel on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ectlogger) is where the recordings go. The series follows the same three paths as this site, so every video has a page here to go back to for the detail:

| Track | Who it is for | Planned videos |
|---|---|---|
| **A: Operator basics** | Everyone | Signing in and finding your net; checking in and taking part |
| **B: Net control** | NCS, Logger, Relay | Running your first net; the net control desk; after the net: reports and ICS-309 |
| **C: Net managers** | Whoever owns a schedule | Setting up a recurring schedule; reading your schedule's statistics |

Short clips follow the tracks, one feature each: speed entry with **Bulk add**, stepping up as backup NCS, chat moderation, traffic, authenticated nets, multiple monitors, and working from a phone in the field.

Every video is recorded on a practice copy of ECTLogger with invented stations, never on a real net, so nothing you see belongs to a real operator.

## Slide decks

None yet. Decks for in-person sessions will be posted here as PDFs after each session is given, so a club can reuse one for its own meeting.

## Learning on your own

You don't need to wait for a video:

- **[Start here](/docs/start/)** — three short tutorials that each end with something real on the screen: your first check-in, your first net, your first schedule.
- **Start Walkthrough** in the app's **Help** menu — a few cards on the main screens, for checking into nets or for running them, each linking to its page here.
- **[Operators](/docs/operators/)**, **[Net control](/docs/net-control/)**, and **[Net managers](/docs/net-managers/)** — the complete paths, when you know what you want to do.

## Resources for making training

The material used to build this training is public, so a club presenting ECTLogger to its own members can reuse it:

- **[Video kit](https://github.com/bradbrownjr/ectlogger/tree/main/docs/training/video-kit)** — title cards, chapter markers, button callouts, lower thirds, end cards, and YouTube thumbnails, as transparent images ready to drop into OBS. Its README explains how to change the wording and rebuild them.
- **[Recording plan](https://github.com/bradbrownjr/ectlogger/blob/main/docs/concepts/TRAINING-VIDEOS.md)** — the outline of every video, the help pages each one follows, and the checklist for staging the practice copy before recording. It is a working plan, so it changes as videos are made.
