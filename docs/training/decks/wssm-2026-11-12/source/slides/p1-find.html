Sign in and find your net

No password needed

Enter your email and click the sign-in link we send

Star your regulars

Starred schedules stay at the top in Favorites

Getting in is easy. Go to app.ectlogger.us, type your email address, and we’ll send you a link. Click it and you’re signed in, with no password to remember. You can set one up later in your profile if you’d rather. The Nets page shows what’s on the air right now and what’s coming up. If there’s a net you check into every week, star its schedule and it stays at the top of the list.
