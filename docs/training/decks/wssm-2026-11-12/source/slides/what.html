The product

A net log everyone sees, live

One list, on every screen

Check-ins appear for everyone the moment they’re logged

Nothing to install

A web page on your phone, tablet or computer

The paperwork writes itself

Report, ICS-309 and log email when the net closes

ECTLogger is a real-time net logger. Net control logs a station, and everybody watching the net sees it at the same moment: who’s checked in, who has traffic, who stepped away. It runs in a web browser, so there’s nothing to install, and when the net closes, the report, the ICS-309 and the log email are ready to go. It’s free and open source, MIT licensed, and the code is on GitHub.
