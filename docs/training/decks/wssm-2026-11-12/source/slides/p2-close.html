When the net’s done, Close net stops check-ins and chat. You can send the log right away, or choose Finish the log later if you still have offline logs or paper notes to add. The log isn’t emailed until you say it’s ready. Afterward, the report and the ICS-309 are one click each, and the report leads with your net’s own logo. The Stats page shows how turnout is trending and who your regulars are.
