Pop panels out onto a second monitor

A big ARES or SKYWARN net often runs on more than one frequency or mode at once: the repeater, a simplex frequency, a DMR talkgroup, maybe HF. In ECTLogger, that’s still one net. Each frequency is a chip in the header, and each NCS clicks the one they’re working to claim it. Their color follows them into the check-in list. We do support multiple NCS covering different repeaters or modes, all logged to the same net, event or incident. A Relay can check in the stations net control can’t hear directly. And if you have a second monitor, you can pop the check-in list, chat or map out onto it.
