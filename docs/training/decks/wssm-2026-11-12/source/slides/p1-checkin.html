Part 1 · Joining a net

Check in and take part

Press Check in

Or answer the prompt when you open a running net

Set your status

Raise hand, Step away, or Just listening

Check out when done

Your row greys out on every screen

To check in, open the net and press Check in, or just answer the prompt that pops up when you arrive. Once you’re on the list, you can raise your hand to get net control’s attention, step away for a few minutes, or mark yourself as just listening if you’re only monitoring. When you’re done, press Check out, and your row greys out for everyone.
