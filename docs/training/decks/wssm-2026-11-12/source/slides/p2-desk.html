Part 2 · Running a club net

At the net control desk

Open lobby, then Start net

Stations gather and check in before you begin

Check in as NCS or Logger

Net staff take the desk just by checking in

Log it like a contact

Callsign, Tab, Enter, just like N3FJP or N1MM

When it’s time, Open lobby lets stations gather and check themselves in before the net begins, and Start net kicks it off. They’re two separate buttons. If you’re on the net’s staff, check in as NCS or as Logger and the desk is yours. A net can have more than one NCS, and stepping away hands off cleanly. Logging a station who calls in on the air is like logging a contact in N3FJP or N1MM. Type the callsign in the row under the list and press Tab. If they have an account or you’ve logged them before, their name and location fill in. Press Enter, and they’re on everyone’s screen, with the cursor back in the Callsign box for the next one.
