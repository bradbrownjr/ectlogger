Part 2 · Running a club net

At the net control desk

Open lobby, then Start net

Stations gather and check in before you begin

Check in as NCS or Logger

Net staff take the desk just by checking in

Log stations fast

Type a callsign and go, or Bulk add a whole list

When it’s time, Open lobby lets stations gather and check themselves in before the net begins, and Start net kicks it off. They’re two separate buttons. If you’re on the net’s staff, check in as NCS or as Logger and the desk is yours. A net can have more than one NCS, and stepping away hands off cleanly. For stations calling in on the air, type the callsign and details and press Enter. If a bunch come in at once, Bulk add takes the whole list in one go.
