A few more tools for exercises and real events. Traffic handles formal ICS-213 messages. The off-net log records contacts a station made away from the net, like a Winlink message, so they still land on the ICS-309. Admins can add the check-in fields your team needs, like a SKYWARN spotter number or a weather report. Authenticated nets let net control confirm a station is who they say they are, using the code from their authenticator app. Drill plans let you lay out the exercise and prepare its messages ahead of time. And if a net ever gets unruly, net control can mute a station’s chat for everyone, or remove and block a station.
