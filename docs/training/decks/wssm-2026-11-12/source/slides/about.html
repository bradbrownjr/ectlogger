A quick bit about me. I’m Brad, KC1JMH, from Waterboro. I’ve been a WSSM member since 2018, and I’ve served in almost every officer position in the club. I’m the Emergency Coordinator, or EC, for the club’s ARES team, the WSSM Emergency Communications Team, which meets right here in the CCEMA bunker on the fourth Thursday. I’ve run net control for club nets, emcomm team nets, SKYWARN, and SET drills. By day I’m a systems administrator: servers, networks, and cloud applications. I started building ECTLogger in November 2025 because I wasn’t satisfied with the options out there, and I kept adding everything I’d ever wished I could do with net logging. The first net went on the air that December.
