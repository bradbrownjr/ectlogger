One-time net?

The + button on Nets sets one up in a minute

For a weekly club net, you set up a recurring schedule once, and ECTLogger creates each net for you ahead of time with its frequencies, script and settings. Add your net staff and an NCS rotation, and everyone can see whose week it is. If someone can’t make it, swapping is a couple of clicks. Reminder emails go to the NCS on duty and to anyone who subscribed. And if a net is just a one-time thing, the + button on the Nets page sets it up in about a minute. A new account needs to be around for a bit, and check into a net or two, before it can create one. Just ask me if you need that sooner.
