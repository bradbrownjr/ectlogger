Here’s what’s coming. The big one for teams like ours is team management: the roster, readiness, equipment and callouts in one place, so no more giant spreadsheets. Work on it has started. Next is working offline, so net control can keep logging when the internet drops, and everything syncs when it comes back. Also on the list: public service events like parades and races, with posts, volunteer shifts and tactical callsigns; an incident log for a whole activation, so a report you hear is written once instead of on paper, in a spreadsheet, in chat and again on the ICS-214; adding a net to your own calendar in one click; and running a net over packet radio or from a text terminal. None of these have dates yet. If one matters to you, tell me through Submit Feedback.
