Part 3 · ARES and SKYWARN

Who can hear whom

Stations press I hear

And pick the stations they can copy

One-way or two-way

Station Coverage sorts it out as reports arrive

Sites, not just stations

Shelters and the EOC, and who’s staffing each

For a simplex drill, the big question is who can hear whom without the repeater. Stations press I hear and pick the stations they can copy. The Station Coverage panel works out which paths are one-way and which are two-way as the reports come in, and it can show them on the map. For an exercise like the SET, you can also set up sites, like shelters and the EOC, check stations in at a site, and see at a glance which sites are staffed and which can reach each other.
