Keeping the net orderly

Participants only

Asks visitors to listen instead of checking in

Mute chat

Just for you, or net control mutes it for everyone

Remove and block

Off the log, muted, and can’t check back in

Every so often someone shows up to cause trouble, and how to handle it is one of the most common questions I get. If a net is for a drill or a team, the net manager can mark it Participants only. It gets a red label, and the check-in form asks everyone else to listen without checking in. Anyone can mute a station’s chat in their own view. Net control can shift-click that same mute button to hide the station’s messages from everyone, and the station isn’t told. If they keep at it, Remove and block takes them off the log, mutes their chat, and stops them checking themselves back in on this net. The net manager can also turn off self check-in, so every check-in goes through net control.
