Chat, the map, and reminders

Put yourself on the map

An address, grid square or coordinates in Location

Never miss a net

Subscribe to a schedule for an email reminder

Chat runs right beside the net. You can reply to a message, @mention a station that’s checked in, or paste in a picture. If someone’s getting chatty, you can mute them, and that only changes your own view. Put an address, grid square or coordinates in Location and you’ll show up on the net’s map. The Schedule page has a calendar of every net coming up, and if you subscribe to one, you’ll get an email reminder before each net.
